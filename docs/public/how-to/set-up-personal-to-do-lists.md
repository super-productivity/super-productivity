# Set up personal to-do lists

> **How-to guide**: Shows how to keep a shopping list, household chores, and a study plan in Super Productivity.
> Use it when you want everyday lists and none of the work features.

Each list below takes a few minutes and needs only the features that a new install starts with.
If you do not track time, [turn off time tracking](turn-off-features-you-do-not-need.md) first.

## Keep a shopping list

1. In the side menu, click **Create project** next to **Projects**, and name the project `Shopping`.
2. Open `Shopping` and add one task per item, such as `Milk` and `Bread`.
3. In the shop, mark each item done: click its circle, press `D`, or swipe it right on a touch screen.
4. At home, click **Move completed tasks to archive** below the list.

The items have no date, so they stay in `Shopping` and never crowd Today.

To keep the whole list in one task instead, add a task `Groceries` and write the items into its notes as a checklist,
one `- [ ] Milk` line per item.
[Task notes](https://super-productivity.com/docs/explanation/task-notes/) describes how the checklist behaves.

## Repeat household chores

1. Create a project `Home`, or add the chores to the Inbox.
2. Type the chore into the add-task bar, such as `Water the plants`.
3. Click **Repeat** in the add-task bar and choose a rhythm, such as **Every day** or **Every week on** a weekday.
4. Press `Enter` to add the task.

On each day the chore is due, a new copy of it appears in Today.

Some chores fall due a set time after you last did them, such as cleaning a filter.
For those, add the task without a repeat, open its details, click **Schedule Task**, and then click **Repeat**.
In **Make task recurring**, choose **After I complete it…** under **Repeats**.
[Manage repeating tasks](https://super-productivity.com/docs/how-to/manage-repeating-tasks/) covers every repeat option.

## Plan a study course

1. Create a project for the course, such as `Statistics exam`.
2. Add one task per chapter.
   For the steps inside a chapter, open the task's details and click **Add subtask**.
3. Add a task `Exam`, open its details, click **Set deadline**, and pick the exam day.
4. For each chapter, open its details, click **Schedule Task**, and pick the day you want to study it.

Each chapter appears in Today on the day you picked, and **Planner** shows the chapters day by day.
A chapter that you leave undone moves to **From previous days** at the top of Today.
On the exam day, `Exam` moves into Today unless you planned it for a later day.

## Check the result

Open **Today**.
It lists the chores due today and the chapters planned for today, and nothing from `Shopping`.
