# Time-block your day

> **How-to guide**: Shows how to give each of today's tasks a slot on a time grid in Super Productivity.
> Use it when a list alone does not tell you whether the day's work fits into your hours.

The Schedule is off on a new install, so the first step switches it on.

## Turn on the Schedule

1. Open **Settings → General**.
2. Under **App Features**, switch on **Schedule**.

**Schedule** appears in the side menu, and a **Schedule** button appears in the header, or in the bottom bar on a phone.

## Estimate the tasks

The Schedule sizes each task by its estimate.

- For a new task, end the title with the estimate, such as `Write report 1h` or `Reply to emails 30m`,
  or click **Estimate** in the add-task bar.
- For an existing task, open its details and set the estimate there.

## Place the tasks on the grid

1. Open **Today**.
2. Click the **Schedule** button.
   The time grid of today opens next to your task list.
3. Drag a task from the list onto the hour where you want to start it.

The task now has a start time, and the app reminds you when it starts.
A task without an estimate takes 15 minutes on the grid.
To pick a time while you type, add it to the title with `@`, such as `Call the bank @16:00`.
On a phone, the grid opens over the list, so pick the time in the task's details with **Schedule Task** instead.

## Let the app place the rest

The app places today's tasks without a start time into the free time of the grid.
It keeps them inside your work hours, 9:00 to 17:00 unless you change them.

To change this, open **Settings → Time & Tracking** and go to **Schedule**:

- Set **Work Day Start** and **Work Day End** to your hours.
- Set **Tasks without a time** to **Kept in a tray** to place each task by hand instead.

## See the week

Open **Schedule** in the side menu, and switch between the day, week, and month views to see the coming days.

## Check the result

The time grid shows each of today's tasks as a block as long as its estimate.
