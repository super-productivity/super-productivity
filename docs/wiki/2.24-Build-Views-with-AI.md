# Build Views with AI

Say or type the view you want, such as a kanban board, an Eisenhower matrix or a weekly review, and the built-in **AI View Builder** plugin has Claude arrange it while you watch. The view shows your real tasks and stays up to date. Keep changing it with follow-up requests like "group by project" or "add today's tracked time on top". The plugin is off by default and optional.

## What you need

- An Anthropic API key from the [Claude Console](https://platform.claude.com/). Each request is billed to that account.
- An internet connection while you change a view. Saved views keep working offline.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings** → **Plugins**.
2. Under **Other plugins**, enable **AI View Builder**.
3. Open **AI View Builder** from the main menu.
4. Paste your API key and click **Save key**. The key is stored on this device only; repeat this on each device where you want to change views.
5. Optionally pick a model in **Settings**. **Claude Opus 5.5** gives the best results; **Sonnet** and **Haiku** answer faster.

## Build and change a view

1. Type what you want into the box and press **Go**, or pick a suggestion.
2. The view builds up on screen as Claude answers; a sentence below the box says what changed. **Stop** cancels and keeps the view as it was.
3. To change only one part, click its title first, then describe the change ("only high priority here"). Press **Escape** to clear the selection.
4. **Undo** and **Redo** (or Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z) step through your changes instantly. Saying or typing "undo" works too.
5. "New view" and "switch to _name_" work without Claude. Use the selector at the top to switch views, and **Settings** → **Delete this view** to remove one.

### Speak instead of typing

- **Web app:** click the microphone and speak; the request is sent when you stop. If your browser can recognize speech on the device it does, otherwise it asks once before sending your voice to the browser's speech service.
- **Desktop and mobile apps:** use your system's dictation in the box (macOS: press Fn twice; Windows: Win+H; on phones, the keyboard's microphone).

## Use a view inside a project or tag

Click the **AI view** button in the header of a project, a tag or Today to show your view in place of the task list there; click it again to switch back. A view that shows "what I have open" adapts to whichever project or tag you are in.

## Working with tasks in a view

- Check a task off, start or stop time tracking, or click its title to open its details.
- On a board, drag a card to another column, or focus a card and press Alt+Left or Alt+Right. Moving a card adds the column's tags, removes the tags the column excludes and sets it done or not done, the same way the built-in boards work. A tag that does not exist yet is created the first time.

Claude never changes your tasks. It only changes how the view shows them; every change to a task comes from you.

## Privacy

- Sent to Anthropic: your request and the layout of the view you are changing. Your tasks, projects, tags and notes are never sent; the view reads them on your device.
- Voice in the web app stays on your device when the browser supports on-device recognition. Otherwise the browser's speech service receives it, after you agree once.
- Your API key is stored like other plugin secrets: on this device only, never synced, exported or backed up.

## Limits

- Views are built from boards, task lists, numbers and short texts. Ask for something else and Claude does the closest thing it can and says what is missing.
- Projects and tags are matched by name, so renaming one makes the view show a note until you mention the new name.
- Views made by a newer version of the plugin open read-only until you update.
