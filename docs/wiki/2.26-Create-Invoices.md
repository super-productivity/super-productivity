# Create Invoices

The built-in **Invoicing** plugin turns the time you tracked on a project into an invoice you can print or save as PDF. It remembers which hours are already billed, so the next invoice only contains new time. Everything stays on your device and syncs with your other data; nothing is sent anywhere.

Each project is billed as a whole: all time tracked on its tasks counts as billable. Keep non-billable work in a separate project.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings** → **Plugins**.
2. Enable **Invoicing**. It is off by default.
3. Open **Invoicing** in the sidebar.

## Enter your details (once)

Expand **Your details** and fill in:

- **Your name, address and tax ID**: printed as the sender, line breaks kept.
- **Payment details**: bank details or payment terms, printed at the bottom.
- **Currency code** (for example `EUR`, `USD`), **Tax / VAT %** (0 hides the tax line), **Days until due**.
- **Round each line up to**: no rounding, or 6, 15 or 30 minutes per line.
- **Invoice number prefix**: for example `2026-` gives `2026-001`.

Click **Save**.

## Create an invoice

1. Under **New invoice**, pick the **Project** and the period (**From** / **To**; defaults to last month).
2. Enter the **Hourly rate**, **Bill to** (client name and address) and the **Invoice language**: the language of the printed headings, dates and amounts (defaults to the app language). All three are remembered for the project.
3. Check the lines: one per top-level task with unbilled time in the period (sub-task time is included in its parent). Untick a line to leave it out, or edit its description or hours. Lowering the hours of a ticked line writes off the rest: all of that line's time counts as billed.
4. Check the **Invoice number** and **Invoice date**, then click **Issue and print**. If the button is greyed out, the text next to it says what is missing.
5. In the print dialog, choose **Save as PDF** to get a file. Where the dialog suggests a file name, it is the invoice number and client. In a web browser, also turn off **Headers and footers**, or the page address and date are printed on the invoice.

The invoice is saved as issued: later changes to tracked time, your details or the rate do not change it. Its hours count as billed; time tracked later on the same tasks and days shows up on the next invoice.

## Manage issued invoices

Issued invoices are listed at the top, newest first:

- **Print / PDF** prints the invoice again.
- **CSV** downloads its lines for an accounting tool.
- **Delete** removes it; its hours count as unbilled again.

## Limits

- Printing needs the desktop app or a desktop browser; the Android app cannot print.
- Invoice numbers continue from the highest issued number. If you issue invoices on two devices before they sync, both can get the same number. Delete one and issue it again with a new number.
- Billed hours belong to the task they were tracked on. Turning a billed sub-task into a top-level task, or moving a billed task under another task, makes its hours show as unbilled again; untick that line.
- One tax rate and one currency per invoice; no expenses, payments or e-invoice formats.
