# Week View

The **Week** view shows today and the next six logical days in a vertical date
grouping. It combines tasks from all active projects, and each task shows its
project so you can see the week across project boundaries.

Tasks are placed on a day using their planned or due fields. A specific due
time takes precedence over a day-only due date, using the app's logical-day
boundary. Tasks without a planned or due day are not shown in the Week view.

The Week view is a compact look ahead. Use the [[4.03-Planner-View]] for
extended scheduling and arranging work across a longer planning horizon.
