# Use Android Home Screen Widgets

The Android app has two home screen widgets:

- **Today's Tasks** lists up to 20 tasks from the Today view. Tap a checkbox to
  mark a task done or not done.
- **Live Tracking** shows the task you are tracking time on and the device
  tracking it, with a **Stop** button.

## Add a Widget

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen and choose **Widgets**.
2. Find **Super Productivity** and drag **Today's Tasks** or **Live Tracking**
   onto the home screen.
3. Resize it if needed. **Live Tracking** fits in a single row.

A new widget uses the app's theme colors and follows your system's light or dark
mode. On Android 11 and older, the appearance screen opens when you add the
widget. Press back to keep the theme colors.

## Change the Widget Background

On Android 12 and newer:

1. Long-press the widget and choose its settings or edit option. The name
   depends on your launcher.
2. Pick a color and set the transparency.
3. Tap **Save**. To follow the system theme again, tap **Use theme default**.

Text and icons switch between dark and light to stay readable on the color you
choose. Each widget keeps its own background.

## Stop Tracking from the Widget

Tap **Stop** on the **Live Tracking** widget to stop time tracking on this
device. Tracking stops right away, even when the app is closed or removed from
recent apps. The tracked time up to the tap is saved to the task when the app
next runs.

The widget only updates while the app can run. If it shows an older state, open
the app.

## Show Tracking from Other Devices

With SuperSync and **Show live tracking on other devices** enabled, the widget
also shows a task tracked on another device. See
[[3.08-Sync-Integration-Comparison]] for this setting.

- Tracking on another device is display-only. The widget has no **Stop** button
  for it. Stop it from the app's header chip or the Android notification.
- The start time ("since 2:15 PM") and the focus session number appear only while
  this setting is enabled, also for tracking on this device. Without it, the
  widget shows the task and device name only.
